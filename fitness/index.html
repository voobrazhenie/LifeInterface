    The plan, at a glance
    

      
Goal: lean gain, not weight loss. Add roughly 3–5 kg of muscle — mostly lateral delts, upper chest and arms — while holding or slightly reducing body fat. Started 29 July 2026; realistic horizon 8–14 months.


      
The four things that waste a year

      
    
        
  • Program hopping — twelve weeks minimum before changing anything
    • 
        
  • Under-eating protein — 1.8–2.2 g per kg per day, every day
    • 
        
  • Never adding weight to the bar — double progression, logged every set
    • 
        
  • Sleeping badly — 7–9 hours, consistent wake time
    • 
      


      
The weekly shape

      
    
        
  • Day 1 — Upper, push emphasis
    • 
        
  • Day 2 — Lower
    • 
        
  • Day 3 — Upper, pull emphasis
    • 
        
  • Day 4 — Weak-point: arms & delts
    • 
      

      
Four sessions a week, 50–60 min. Lateral raises appear on three of the four days — that is deliberate.


      
What to measure, and how often

      
    
        
  • Every session — weight and reps for every set
    • 
        
  • Weekly, Sunday — bodyweight 7-day average, waist at navel, sessions hit, protein days hit
    • 
        
  • Monthly — same-pose photo, saved as NOW - DD.MM.YYYY.jpeg
    • 
      

      
At week 6, "working" looks like lifts up 15–25% and almost no visual change. If the lifts are climbing it is working — trust that over the mirror.
